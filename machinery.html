MACHINERY & PRODUCTION

Where Technology Meets Craftsmanship

Machinery provides the controlled mechanical operations needed to transform prepared metal into useful cutlery components and finished products.

In cutlery manufacturing, different machines support different stages of production. Cutting equipment prepares material, forming equipment creates shapes, grinding refines edges and surfaces, while polishing and buffing equipment support final finishing.

Controlled material processing
Consistent production operations
Surface refinement
Skilled machine operation
MACHINERY DIRECTORY

Explore The Production Floor

Each machine performs a different role in the production journey. Explore the detailed function, working principle, material, production stage and output of each machine.

01
CUTTING
MATERIAL PREPARATION

Sheet Cutting Machine

Sheet cutting machines are used to divide stainless-steel sheets or prepared metal stock into smaller production pieces. Proper material preparation is an important early step because the size and shape of the prepared piece affects later forming operations.

How It Works

The stainless-steel sheet is positioned according to the required cutting dimensions. The cutting mechanism then separates the sheet into suitable production pieces. The exact cutting method depends on the machine design, material thickness and workshop requirements.

PRIMARY FUNCTION Sheet Cutting
MATERIAL Stainless Steel
PRODUCTION STAGE Initial Preparation
OUTPUT Cut Components
MAIN BENEFIT

Controlled material preparation for subsequent manufacturing operations.

02
FORMING
METAL FORMING

Hydraulic Press Machine

Hydraulic presses use controlled pressure to shape prepared metal components. In manufacturing environments, suitable tooling can be used to create curves, forms and shapes required for different cutlery components.

How It Works

A prepared metal piece is placed in suitable tooling. Hydraulic pressure moves the press mechanism and applies force to the material. The tooling and operating conditions depend on the particular forming operation.

PRIMARY FUNCTION Metal Forming
MATERIAL Stainless Steel
PRODUCTION STAGE Shaping
OUTPUT Formed Components
MAIN BENEFIT

Supports controlled and repeatable forming operations.

03
FORMING
MATERIAL SHAPING

Rolling Machine

Rolling equipment is used in metalworking operations to shape or prepare metal material. Depending on the machine design, rolling can influence the form or dimensions of material before further production operations.

How It Works

Metal passes between rollers that apply controlled mechanical force. As the material moves through the rollers, its form or dimensions can be modified according to the intended operation.

PRIMARY FUNCTION Material Shaping
MATERIAL Metal Stock
PRODUCTION STAGE Preparation
OUTPUT Prepared Material
MAIN BENEFIT

Helps prepare metal material for later forming operations.

04
GRINDING
SURFACE REFINEMENT

Grinding Machine

Grinding machines refine metal components after cutting and forming. Grinding can remove unwanted material, reduce rough areas and prepare edges or surfaces for subsequent finishing operations.

How It Works

An abrasive wheel, belt or another suitable grinding surface contacts the metal component. Controlled abrasion removes small amounts of material and helps produce a more refined edge or surface.

PRIMARY FUNCTION Surface Refinement
MATERIAL Stainless Steel
PRODUCTION STAGE Grinding
OUTPUT Refined Component
MAIN BENEFIT

Prepares edges and surfaces for later finishing operations.

05
POLISHING
SURFACE FINISHING

Polishing Machine

Polishing machines improve the smoothness and appearance of suitable metal surfaces. For cutlery, this finishing stage is particularly important because the visible surface contributes strongly to the final product appearance.

How It Works

The metal component is brought into contact with a suitable polishing wheel or finishing medium. Controlled finishing action improves the surface according to the required product finish.

PRIMARY FUNCTION Surface Polishing
MATERIAL Stainless Steel
PRODUCTION STAGE Finishing
OUTPUT Polished Surface
MAIN BENEFIT

Improves surface smoothness and final visual appearance.

06
FINISHING
FINAL SURFACE FINISH

Buffing Machine

Buffing equipment supports fine surface finishing after grinding and polishing. It can improve surface smoothness and contribute to a refined appearance on suitable stainless-steel surfaces.

How It Works

A rotating buffing wheel works with an appropriate finishing compound or medium. The operator carefully presents the component to the wheel to achieve the required surface finish.

PRIMARY FUNCTION Fine Finishing
MATERIAL Stainless Steel
PRODUCTION STAGE Final Finish
OUTPUT Finished Surface
MAIN BENEFIT

Supports fine finishing and enhances product appearance.

PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

From Steel To Finished Cutlery

Machinery supports multiple stages of production while skilled workers operate, monitor and inspect the process.

Material

02

Cutting

03

Forming

04

Grinding

05

Polishing
