About TEVTA Training

Technical Skills for Practical Industry

Technical and vocational education focuses on practical knowledge and skills that can help learners understand tools, machinery, materials, workshop procedures and industrial safety.

The Cutlery & Small Tools Industries Service Centre in Wazirabad provides an important local connection between technical skills and the area's manufacturing environment.
