01 — INDUSTRY

About the Cutlery Industry

The cutlery and manufacturing industry produces a wide range of kitchen, table, professional and specialty cutlery products. It combines traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing techniques to produce durable and high-quality products.

Skilled Craftsmanship

Skilled workers play an important role in cutting, forging, grinding and polishing cutlery products.

Modern Machinery

Manufacturing machines improve production efficiency, accuracy and finishing quality.

Quality Products

Quality control helps manufacturers produce reliable, durable and attractive cutlery products.

Pakistan's Cutlery Industry

Pakistan has a growing manufacturing sector with established expertise in metal products and cutlery. Manufacturers produce different types of kitchen, table, professional and specialty cutlery.

The industry provides opportunities for skilled workers, technical trainees, manufacturers and businesses.
