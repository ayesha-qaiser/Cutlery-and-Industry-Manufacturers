HOW CUTLERY IS MADE

Precision At Every Stage

Quality cutlery is created through a series of carefully controlled manufacturing stages. At Cutlery Hub, each stage contributes to the shape, strength, appearance and final finish of the product.

From preparing stainless-steel material to cutting, forming, grinding, polishing and final inspection, the manufacturing journey combines technical processes with skilled craftsmanship.

STAGE 01

Selecting The Right Material

The manufacturing process begins with stainless-steel material selected according to the requirements of the final product.

Material preparation is an important first step because the quality and characteristics of the material influence the later forming, finishing and polishing stages.

STAGE 02

Cutting & Preparing The Components

Stainless-steel sheets are cut into appropriate sizes and shapes according to the product design.

Accurate cutting helps prepare the material for the next manufacturing stages and supports consistent product dimensions.

PRECISION Accurate component preparation
CONSISTENCY Uniform production pieces
STAGE 03

Shaping & Forming The Cutlery

Once the material is prepared, it is formed into the basic shape of the required cutlery product.

Different forming techniques are used for spoons, forks, knives and other cutlery pieces to achieve their required dimensions and profile.

01 Spoon forming
02 Fork shaping

STAGE 04

Grinding & Edge Preparation

After forming, the pieces may require grinding and edge preparation to remove unwanted material and refine the product shape.

This stage prepares the surface and edges for further finishing and polishing.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

Careful grinding helps create a clean and refined foundation for the finishing stages.

STAGE 05

Polishing For A Refined Finish

Polishing is one of the most important finishing stages. The surface is refined to achieve the smooth appearance associated with professionally finished stainless-steel cutlery.
